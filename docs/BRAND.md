# Mira

Mira is a Link Intelligence platform that turns distributed URLs into programmable, measurable and durable infrastructure.

The name is short and natural in Portuguese and English. It carries two useful meanings: to look closely and to aim. That mirrors the product: understand traffic, then direct it deliberately.

Primary promise: **See what moves. Direct what matters.**

Mira is not positioned as a URL shortener. The preferred category is **Link Intelligence**, followed by Programmable Links, Campaign Intelligence and Branded Link Infrastructure.

The visual identity uses ink for reliability, warm ivory to avoid sterile dashboard aesthetics, and signal green only for action, live state and verified progress. The intersecting-ring mark represents destination and outcome: connected, but independently visible.

Voice is precise, calm and concrete. Mira explains what happened, what is known, the confidence of an inference and the next useful action.

An initial public category search was completed on 24 August 2026. Formal trademark clearance and definitive domain acquisition require specialist validation before general availability.
